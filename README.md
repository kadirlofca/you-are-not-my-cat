## 🐾 Mini Jam Submission

**This game is submitted for Mini Jam #154 on itch.io.**  

🎮 **Play the game here:** [You Are Not My Cat!](https://haft-indie.itch.io/you-are-not-my-cat)  

---

## 🏠 Gameplay

- Collect furniture and items around the house to decorate your box house.  
- Avoid being caught by the homeowner.  
- Customize your box house by placing collected items inside.  

---

## ⌨️ Controls

| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Move | WASD + Space |
| Exit Menu | ESC |
| Move Items Inside Box | Left Mouse + Drag |
